# Principles

[Русская версия](PRINCIPLES.ru.md)

1. **Independence.** Ratings, verdicts, and editorial conclusions cannot be purchased.
2. **Official sources first.** Primary evidence has priority over summaries and marketing directories.
3. **Facts before opinions.** Verified facts, observations, analysis, and unresolved questions must be separated.
4. **Unknown means unknown.** Never fill missing information with plausible assumptions.
5. **Comparison over description.** Atlas must add trade-offs, alternatives, and decision context.
6. **Quality over quantity.** A small maintained collection is better than thousands of stale summaries.
7. **Claims must be traceable.** Important claims require evidence and a verification date.
8. **Dates are part of the fact.** Pricing, limits, versions, and availability change.
9. **Task-oriented recommendations.** There is rarely one universal winner.
10. **Transparent scoring.** Scores require public criteria, traceable inputs, and explicit handling of missing data.
11. **Preserve provenance.** Useful historical research should be migrated or archived, not silently destroyed.
12. **Build for use.** Every maintained document must help a reader, researcher, contributor, or automated workflow.
