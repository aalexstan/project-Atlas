# CDEK logistics API research

## Scope

Review the current official CDEK developer and business integration pages. This profile concerns direct logistics integration, not CDEK Fulfillment or CDEK Pay.

## Official sources reviewed

- [CDEK developer portal](https://developer.cdek.ru/)
- [CDEK business delivery](https://partners.cdek.ru/)
- [CDEK API documentation portal](https://apidoc.cdek.ru/)

## Confirmed facts

- CDEK maintains a developer portal with a logistics-integration area described as delivery-process automation and waybill management.
- The portal describes ready integrations that automate delivery calculation, waybill creation, status synchronisation, pickup-point selection and document printing.
- CDEK’s business page states that customers can use direct API integration or individual development for non-standard tasks.
- CDEK describes business delivery across Russia and more than 40 countries on its reviewed business page.

## Unknowns and blockers

- The reviewed accessible pages did not provide a captured current endpoint catalog, authentication model, schemas, versioning, rate limits, SLA or API-specific price.
- Storage, customer display, SaaS, redistribution and personal-data terms are unknown.
- No credentials, sandbox request or live shipment was used.

## Decision

Create a reviewed logistics-integration profile with explicit unknown API mechanics. Add it to the delivery comparison as a contract-based carrier integration route.
