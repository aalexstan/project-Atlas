# Bank of Russia Exchange Rates Research Log

[Русская версия](2026-08-23.ru.md)

## Scope

Assess whether the Bank of Russia's official exchange-rate XML resources constitute a bounded API-first profile for financial and reporting use cases.

## Official sources reviewed

- https://www.cbr.ru/development/
- https://www.cbr.ru/development/SXML/
- https://www.cbr.ru/scripts/XML_daily.asp
- https://www.cbr.ru/scripts/XML_val.asp?d=0

## Confirmed facts

- The Bank of Russia maintains a technical-resources section for integrating information systems.
- The SXML documentation describes `XML_daily.asp` and the optional `date_req` date parameter.
- The same documentation lists currency-code XML resources.
- The official technical-resources page describes the web-service system as free and publicly accessible.

## Provider-reported claims

- Public/free availability is recorded as provider-reported; Atlas did not perform a request or credentials-based test.

## Unknowns and blockers

- No public operational rate limits or SLA were established.
- Commercial storage, redistribution, attribution and SaaS terms were not established.
- The review does not establish real-time market quotes, trading data or execution capabilities.

## Live testing

Not performed.

## Decision

Create an active `verified` profile for the bounded daily exchange-rate XML web service. Keep the product class and limitations explicit; do not represent it as a general market-data API.
