## Confirmed facts

- T-Bank publishes a developer portal with a separate internet acquiring section and OpenAPI-related materials.
- Official business documentation describes API integration for websites, applications, CRM and IT systems.
- The internet acquiring route supports payment, confirmation/hold, cancellation, refund and recurring-payment scenarios according to the official business documentation.
- Webhooks are documented for payment status and other T-API events; enabling a webhook requires provider-side connection steps.
- T-Bank states that internet acquiring has no subscription fee and that payment commissions are calculated individually; the official page also states a fixed fee for an unsuccessful authorization.
- The provider requires a registered business and onboarding/application for internet acquiring.

## Provider-reported claims

- T-Bank lists cards, T-Pay, SberPay, Alfa Pay, Mir Pay, SBP, payment links, and other methods in its online acquiring materials.
- The provider describes settlement timing and operating conditions in business help pages; exact conditions depend on the merchant setup and agreement.

## Unknowns and blockers

- Merchant-specific commission table, minimum fees, settlement terms, production quotas, and SLA.
- Exact public API base URL and method-level contract for the selected acquiring integration.
- Storage, caching, customer display, SaaS, redistribution, and cross-border use terms.
- Whether every T-API capability listed in the portal is available under the same onboarding and legal product boundary.
