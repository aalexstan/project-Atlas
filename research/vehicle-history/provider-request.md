# Avtocod B2B API provider request

[Русская версия](provider-request.ru.md)

Please provide written answers for the intended API integration:

1. Which report types and source/field matrix are included?
2. What are the per-report prices, minimum commitment, package rollover, overage and regeneration charges?
3. What daily, monthly, total and frequency quotas will be configured?
4. What numerical availability SLA, support response times, incident communication and service credits apply?
5. What source freshness, correction, dispute and data-deletion processes apply?
6. May reports be stored outside Avtocod, cached, displayed to customers, exported, redistributed or resold?
7. May the data be embedded in SaaS or shared with affiliates and end customers?
8. Are automated insurance, lending, leasing or fraud decisions allowed? What human-review and audit requirements apply?
9. Is model training, feature engineering or statistical scoring allowed?
10. What personal-data roles, DPA, retention, localization and security obligations apply?
11. Is a non-billable sandbox or synthetic report type available?
12. What changelog, schema-version and breaking-change notification policy applies?
