# Evidence

[Русская версия](evidence.ru.md)

- Avtocod documents HTTPS GET/POST, JSON responses, base URL and Swagger UI: [overview](https://b2bapi.avtocod.ru/docs/about/overview).
- The quick start documents account onboarding, token use, balance checks and report generation/retrieval: [quick start](https://b2bapi.avtocod.ru/docs/using-api/quick-start).
- Report-field specifications, JSON Schema and an example report are public: [specifications](https://b2bapi.avtocod.ru/docs/reference/specs).
- Daily, monthly and total quotas, HTTP `402`, report-type frequency limits and HTTP `429` are documented: [quotas](https://b2bapi.avtocod.ru/docs/using-api/quote), [errors](https://b2bapi.avtocod.ru/docs/reference/errors).
- Reports are normally available for six months; reads are free while forced regeneration is paid. Webhook delivery is not guaranteed: [report management](https://b2bapi.avtocod.ru/docs/using-api/report).
- Public B2B prices include Autofill at 10 RUB and Autofill Plus at 11 RUB per report, with individual terms from 10,000 reports: [business tariffs](https://avtocod.ru/for-business-tariffs).
- Avtocod reports more than 100 sources and markets insurance, lending, leasing, integrator and scoring scenarios. These remain provider claims and do not replace contract/legal review: [business data](https://avtocod.ru/for-business-data), [integrators](https://avtocod.ru/for-business-cooperation-integrators).
- No Atlas live test or paid report was performed.
