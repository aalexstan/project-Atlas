# Commerce Operations Data and Rights Checklist

[Русская версия](CHECKLIST.ru.md)

Confirm in writing:

- Which seller, customer, employee and counterparty fields may be stored.
- Whether raw responses, normalized records and derived analytics may be cached.
- Whether data may be shown to the account owner, their customers or a third party.
- Whether a paid SaaS may serve multiple accounts.
- Whether data may be exported, redistributed, resold or used to train models.
- Retention, deletion, backup and incident-notification requirements.
- Personal-data roles, DPA, subprocessors and cross-border transfer rules.
- Attribution, logo and marketplace-brand requirements.
- Account suspension, token revocation and data retrieval on termination.
- Audit logs, access segregation and encryption responsibilities.

Do not mark a right as confirmed because an endpoint technically returns the field.
