# Payment API Commercial and Legal Checklist

[Русская версия](CHECKLIST.ru.md)

Confirm in writing before selection:

- merchant eligibility and prohibited categories;
- commission by payment method, minimums, failed-payment fees and settlement timing;
- refunds, chargebacks, reserves and reconciliation exports;
- receipts, 54-FZ and responsibility for fiscal data;
- personal data, PCI DSS scope, DPA and incident notification;
- storage, caching, customer display, SaaS, redistribution and deletion;
- production quotas, SLA, support and deprecation notice;
- cross-border payment and currency restrictions;
- whether payment links, recurring payments, payouts and marketplace split are separate contracts.
