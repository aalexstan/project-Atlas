# Messaging API Compliance Checklist

[Русская версия](CHECKLIST.ru.md)

Confirm consent, opt-out and suppression-list handling; sender and brand approval; personal-data and DPA terms; OTP/security obligations; retention and deletion; operator and country restrictions; delivery-report ownership; SLA and incident handling; customer-facing display, SaaS and redistribution rights; and price assumptions including VAT, multipart messages and failed delivery.
