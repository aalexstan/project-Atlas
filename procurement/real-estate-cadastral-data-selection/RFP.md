# Real-estate and Cadastral Data RFP

[Русская версия](RFP.ru.md)

Request written answers for:

- product and legal-output boundary;
- supported methods, fields, identifiers, geometry and history;
- official source and update/freshness process;
- onboarding, authentication, schemas and versioning;
- synchronous, asynchronous and batch operations;
- quotas, rate limits, concurrency and retry behavior;
- extract/API price, package validity, minimum commitment and overage;
- SLA, support and incident communication;
- correction, dispute and source-unavailability handling;
- personal-data and restricted-information legal grounds;
- storage, caching, indexing and customer-facing display;
- SaaS, affiliates, redistribution and resale;
- valuation, scoring, automated decisions and model training;
- changelog, deprecation and breaking-change notice;
- audit evidence and deletion obligations.
