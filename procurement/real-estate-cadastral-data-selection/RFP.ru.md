# RFP на данные о недвижимости и кадастре

[English version](RFP.md)

Запросите письменные ответы по темам:

- границы продукта и юридическая сила результата;
- methods, fields, identifiers, geometry и history;
- official source, updates и freshness;
- onboarding, authentication, schemas и versioning;
- synchronous, asynchronous и batch operations;
- quotas, rate limits, concurrency и retries;
- цена выписки/API, срок пакета, minimum commitment и overage;
- SLA, support и incident communication;
- corrections, disputes и source unavailability;
- legal grounds для personal data и restricted information;
- storage, caching, indexing и customer-facing display;
- SaaS, affiliates, redistribution и resale;
- valuation, scoring, automated decisions и model training;
- changelog, deprecation и breaking-change notice;
- audit evidence и deletion obligations.
