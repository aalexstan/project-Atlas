# Scorecard Methodology

[Русская версия](SCORING.ru.md)

## Principle

Scores are not prefilled opinions. Enter a score only after evidence exists.

## Scale

- **0** — absent or unacceptable;
- **1** — very weak fit;
- **2** — weak;
- **3** — sufficient;
- **4** — strong;
- **5** — excellent fit.

`Unknown` is not zero. Leave Score blank when evidence is unknown.

## Formula

The weighted result uses completed criteria only:

`Σ(weight × score) / Σ(weight of scored criteria)`

Always review **Scored Weight Coverage**. Do not use a result covering less than 75% of total weight.

## Critical Criteria

Critical areas include:

- price transparency and modellability;
- capacity limits;
- mandatory field coverage;
- freshness;
- SLA;
- storage;
- customer display and redistribution;
- breaking changes.

One unresolved critical question may block a decision regardless of total score.

## No Universal Atlas Ranking

The same provider may be:

- best for signup-form autofill;
- too expensive for bulk processing;
- best for enterprise risk control;
- legally unsuitable for SaaS.

The scorecard measures fit for a specific scenario, not abstract global quality.
